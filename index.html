Who are we

Three decades of data processing, prepress and PCB expertise

We have been in the IT and ITES industry since 1990. We started off as a unit providing data processing and software solutions on commercial applications for various industries.

In our journey since 1990, we expanded our services for printing and PCB industry by providing prepress designing, photo plotting, film output and related pre-process services.

We widened further as a solution provider and rendering our services in consulting, interfacing solutions for PCB-CNC machinery, IT enabled solutions and hardware solutions.

Our mission

Our mission is to be the preferred service provider by being agile to changing technology trends and to provide exceptional customer services. Providing the right quality and cost efficiency methods for a long-standing relationship with customers. To provide unmatched services and to become an easy choice for customers.
