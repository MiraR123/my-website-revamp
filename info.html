About the company

Business Automation Centre at a glance

In the IT and ITES industry since 1990 — data processing and software solutions for commercial applications, prepress designing, photo plotting, film output and PCB pre-process services, plus consulting, interfacing solutions for PCB-CNC machinery and hardware solutions.

We are based in Kodambakkam, Chennai, and serve printing and PCB manufacturers across the industry. See our services or our infrastructure.
